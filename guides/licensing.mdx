---
title: "Commercial integrations"
description: "Choose access for internal workflows or customer-facing products."
---

Retail Pro and Business subscriptions support internal use. To show records to your own customers, use [commercial data licensing](https://www.foreclosuredatahub.com/data-licensing).

Commercial keys use the same API endpoints and `x-api-key` header, with separate monthly call allowances. A commercial evaluation includes 50 successful calls over three days, at most 10 records per response, and 10 requests/minute. It does not convert automatically.

Review current tiers and license terms on the [licensing page](https://www.foreclosuredatahub.com/data-licensing), and manage access in the [license dashboard](https://www.foreclosuredatahub.com/dashboard/license).
