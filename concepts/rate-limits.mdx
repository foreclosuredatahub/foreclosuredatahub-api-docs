## Retail request limits

All retail keys on an account share these limits:

| Endpoint | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Search | 15 requests/minute |
| Source data | 30 requests/minute |
| Counts | 15 requests/minute |
| Sources | 10 requests/minute |
| All endpoints combined | 1,000 requests/hour |
| New trial | 30 requests total |

Exceeding a rate limit returns `429` with `Retry-After`.

## Retail row allowances

Pro includes **10,000 rows/month**. Business includes **30,000 shared rows/month** and five people. CSV, Google Sheets, and API property responses share the account's row allowance. Unused rows do not roll over.

Paid property responses return at most **100 rows**, defaulting to 25. New trial responses return at most **10 rows**, shared with the trial's **500 delivered rows**. Sources and counts consume requests but do not consume listing rows.

On the first page, an omitted size may be reduced to the remaining balance. Explicit sizes are never silently reduced. A request above your allowance returns `403 EXPORT_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` with the remaining rows and reset date; request a smaller count explicitly.

Successful property responses include:

- `X-Export-Quota-Limit`
- `X-Export-Quota-Remaining`
- `X-Export-Quota-Reset` (Unix seconds)

View current usage in [Settings → Subscription](https://www.foreclosuredatahub.com/dashboard/settings/subscription).

<Warning>Rows are charged immediately before sending the prepared response. Every new request, including a retry, can consume rows again. If delivery is uncertain, check the result before sending another request.</Warning>

## Commercial limits

Commercial evaluations allow **50 successful calls total over three days**, **10 records per response**, and **10 requests/minute**. They do not reset monthly or convert automatically, and no card is required. Paid commercial licenses use separate monthly call allowances and allow at most 100 records per response. See [commercial licensing](https://www.foreclosuredatahub.com/data-licensing) for current tiers.
